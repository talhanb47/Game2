🖼️ Add Your Own Images & Audio

No programming needed. Put your files into the folders beside index.html, then reopen or refresh the game.

1. Open the images folder and add your pictures, e.g. Happy.png.
2. Open the audio folder and add your voice clips, e.g. Happy.mp3.
3. Use the exact English names shown below (capital first letter).
4. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or another modern browser.

Accepted images: .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .webp, .gif, .svg   |   Accepted audio: .mp3, .wav, .m4a, .ogg, .webm. You may mix extensions, but use only one file per feeling when possible.

audio/BackgroundMusic.mp3

Background music: Put your own BackgroundMusic.mp3 into the audio folder. You can also use .wav, .m4a, .ogg, or .webm. Music loops quietly and starts after you click Start the Game. Use the 🎵 button to turn only music on or off, and the volume slider to adjust it. The 🔊 button still mutes everything.

Note: “Scared” appears twice in your requested round list, so the 10-round game uses Scared twice but needs only one Scared image and one audio recording. Optional success and retry sounds can be added as audio/Correct.mp3 and audio/TryAgain.mp3; the game still works without them.
